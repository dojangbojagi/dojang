Enter with a proof, not a balance.

A smart contract decides who may enter. A valid proof is the key, and the number behind it stays private.

Placeholder page. The full design for this page is the next step. The header, footer, scenarios and the plan below already follow the master brief.

What this page will do

  • Show the active policy, the requirement, proof readiness and the on-chain access state.
  • “Enter Vault” sends a wallet transaction that the contract checks. It is never only a local page change.
  • Walk through the transaction: simulation, wallet confirmation, submitted, confirming, then confirmed, reverted or rejected.
  • Show the explorer link and the access event once the transaction is confirmed.
  • Block the action, or explain the on-chain rejection, when the proof is invalid, expired, revoked, for another wallet, or the network is wrong.

No deposits, no custody, no yield. The vault only records who has access. A green check or an animation is never proof that something happened on-chain: only a confirmed transaction is.
