What this page will do

  • List the official GIWA contracts that are actually used, such as DojangScroll and EAS, with their official addresses and links.
  • List the project’s own contracts: the credential registry, the eligibility verifier and the restricted vault.
  • For each one show the network, address, source verification, role, main read and write functions, deployment transaction and events.
  • Say “Not deployed yet” for anything that is not on-chain. Example addresses are never shown as if they were real.
  • Make real verification and execution transactions easy to find in the explorer.
  • Keep issuer and admin write functions out of reach of visitors who are not authorised.

Nothing here is invented. Addresses, transaction hashes and events appear only after they exist on GIWA Sepolia. Until then each slot says so.
