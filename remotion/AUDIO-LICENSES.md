## License scope

Mixkit’s [Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/) permits free use in commercial and non-commercial projects, including social media video posts and online marketing ads, and allows the music to be incorporated into web and social videos. It excludes TV and radio broadcasts, CDs and DVDs, and video games. It also prohibits redistributing a track as standalone music, claiming it as your own, or registering it with a rights-management service. Check the linked license and Mixkit User Terms for the current full conditions before distribution.

I decoded each track to mono and marked local rises in 50 ms RMS windows (10 ms hop), then placed major scene changes or object accents near the closest markers. These are waveform cues, not a beat-grid transcription, and they do not replace listening.
